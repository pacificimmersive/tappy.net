How to pop bubble wrap online

Tap or click any bubble to pop it. Hold down and drag to pop a bunch in one swipe. When the sheet is done, press Refill for a new one. The counter keeps track of every bubble you’ve popped.

Sound is on for a crisp pop with every bubble, and on many phones you’ll feel a little tap too. Turn sound off if you need to pop quietly.

Why is popping bubble wrap so satisfying?

Popping bubble wrap is a tiny job with an instant payoff: press, pop, done. That quick loop of action and feedback is a big part of why fidget toys feel good. It gives restless hands something to do while you think, wait, or wind down.

And unlike the real thing, this sheet is free, endless, and won’t leave a mess.
