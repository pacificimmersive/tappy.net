What are fidget toys?

Fidget toys are small things you can click, spin, squish, or pop to keep your hands busy. People use them to stay focused in class or meetings, to settle down when they feel restless, or just because they’re fun.

Online fidget toys work the same way, but live on your screen, so you always have one with you.

There are lots of kinds, from Pop Its and spinners to squishies and Newton’s cradles. Our guide to the types of fidget toys walks through them all.

Are online fidgets as good as real ones?

They can’t fully match the feel of a real toy, but they get close, especially with sound on and on phones that vibrate. And they’re free, silent when you need them to be, and impossible to lose.

For the closest thing to the real feel, the Tappy app uses your iPhone’s haptics so each fidget has its own texture.
