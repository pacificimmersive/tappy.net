How to spin the fidget spinner

Drag in a circle around the spinner and let go to flick it. The faster you flick, the faster it spins. Grab it to stop it, or let it slowly wind down on its own.

Your current speed shows in RPM (rotations per minute), and your best spin is saved on this device so you can try to beat it next time.

Why are fidget spinners so satisfying?

A fidget spinner gives you a simple motion to repeat and something smooth to watch: a quick flick, then a long spin that slowly fades. It’s an easy way to keep your hands busy on a call, while studying, or any time you need a moment to reset.
