Why is flipping a light switch so satisfying?

A light switch gives you a crisp, definite click: one small push and something visibly changes. That clean on-and-off feedback is what makes switches, clicky pens, and buttons such popular fidgets.

This one never wears out, and nobody will ask you to stop flicking the lights.

How to use the light switch fidget

Tap the switch to flip it, or swipe up to turn the light on and down to turn it off. You can also use the Space or Enter key. Your flip count is shown at the top of the card.
