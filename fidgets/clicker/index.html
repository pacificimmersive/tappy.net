How to use the online clicker

Tap anywhere on the counter, click it with your mouse, or press Space or Enter. Every press adds one: the plunger clicks down and the numbers roll over, just like a metal tally counter. It counts up to 9999, then rolls back to 0000.

Your count is saved in your browser, so you can close the page and keep counting later. Press Reset to start again from zero. The number at the top shows how many clicks you’ve made in the last second, so you can see how fast you can go.

What is a tally counter?

A tally counter, or hand clicker, is a small metal counter with a button on top and a ring to hold it by. Each press adds one to the number in its little window. People use them to count laps, stitches, people coming through a door, or birds on a walk.

Plenty of people keep one around just for the click. It’s a simple, satisfying press that never runs out.
