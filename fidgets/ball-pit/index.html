How to play the ball pit

Tap anywhere to drop a ball, or hold and drag to pour lots of them. Keep going until the pit is full and the background changes color. Press Clear to empty it and start again.

On a phone, tilt it to roll the balls from side to side. On iPhone, tap Tilt first to let the page use motion.

Why ball pits are so fun to watch

There’s something soothing about watching dozens of balls tumble, bounce, and settle into place. Every pour lands a little differently, so you can keep dropping and watching for as long as you like.

Why did the background change color?

That means the pit is full. From then on, each new ball replaces an old one, so you can keep pouring forever.

How do I tilt the balls?

On Android, just tilt your phone. On iPhone, tap Tilt and allow motion access, then tilt away.
