How to play Pop It online

Tap a bubble to push it in, or drag across a row to pop several at once. When every bubble is popped, the Pop It flips over by itself, just like turning over a real one, and you can start again. Press Flip anytime for a fresh side.

What is a Pop It?

A Pop It is a silicone fidget toy covered in little bubbles that you push in with a soft pop. Once they’re all pushed in, you turn it over and pop them back from the other side. They usually come in rainbow colors and all sorts of shapes. This one is the classic square.

People love them for the same reason they love bubble wrap: each press gives a small, satisfying pop, and you never run out.
