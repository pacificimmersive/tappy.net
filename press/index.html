Full description

Tappy is a sensory fidget and self-care app where every tap, click, and swipe comes alive with satisfying sounds, haptics, and animations. Meet your new self-care pet, who levels up as you relax, breathe, journal, and build healthy habits. Join over a million people who fidget, stim, check in, and unwind with Tappy.

Tappy grows with you as you destress, stay focused, and build a routine that's all yours. Complete a Daily Adventure of quests and collect Sparks to unlock outfits, scenes, and surprises.

And it's all in one app: 25+ sensory fidgets, 9 breathing exercises, 40+ calming soundscapes, a mood journal, daily gratitude, a habit tracker, goal tracking, and 15 arcade games.

What's new in Tappy 6.0

Tappy 6.0 turns Tappy into a self-care pet who grows with you. It adds the Daily Adventure, caring for Tappy with a Care Bar and daily surprise gifts, Sparks and the Shop, new scenes like the beach, a farm, a castle, and outer space, Bedtime, Goals, badges and friendship levels, a Daily Adventure widget, and Live Activities for timed quests.

Using the logo

A few simple rules keep Tappy looking like Tappy.

✓ Please do

  • Use the full-color logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark ones.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as the “T” in Tappy.
  • Scale the logo proportionally.
  • Write the name as “Tappy” with a capital T. The full App Store title is “Tappy: Fidget & Self-Care Pet.”

✕ Please don’t

  • Stretch, squash, or rotate the logo.
  • Recolor the logo or add outlines, shadows, or other effects.
  • Place the logo on busy backgrounds where it’s hard to read.
  • Redraw the logo or combine it with other logos.
