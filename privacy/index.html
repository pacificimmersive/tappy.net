Tappy Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026.

If you are visually impaired, have another disability, or need help accessing this Privacy Policy, email us at cameron@pacific.dev.

The short version

  • Your mood entries, journal, habits, to-dos, drawings, and progress stay on your device and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud account. Pacific Interactive LLC cannot see them and never receives a copy.
  • Tappy has no accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking. We do not use the advertising identifier, we do not fingerprint your device, and we never sell information about you.
  • The only information about you that leaves your device for our benefit is an anonymous purchase record held by our subscription-management provider (currently RevenueCat) so that Tappy Plus and other purchases keep working across your devices.
  • Apple Health integration is off by default. When you turn it on, Tappy writes Mindful Minutes and (with Tappy Plus) State of Mind entries to the Health app on your device. That data never goes to us and is never used for marketing.
  • You are in control. You can export your mood journal as a CSV, lock it with Face ID, revoke any permission, and delete your data by deleting the app and its iCloud data. A small backup of your game progress can stay in your iCloud account; Sections 6.2 and 12 explain it and walk you through deletion.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Pacific Interactive LLC (“Pacific,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a California limited liability company, publishes Tappy, currently titled Tappy: Self Care Pet & Fidget on the App Store. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tappy iPhone and iPad app, the Tappy Apple Watch app, Tappy’s widgets, and the website at tappy.net (together, the “Service”) handle, where that information lives, who can see it, and the choices you have.

This policy applies only to Tappy and tappy.net. Pacific’s other applications have their own privacy policies. If a link inside the App or on a Pacific website points to Pacific’s general privacy policy, this Tappy Privacy Policy controls for Tappy to the extent of any conflict.

For purposes of data-protection laws such as the GDPR, Pacific Interactive LLC is the “controller” of the limited information described in this policy that we actually receive. Information that stays on your device or in your personal iCloud account is under your control and Apple’s, under your agreements with Apple; we do not have access to it.

Tappy is a casual sensory fidget, entertainment, and general wellness companion. It is not a medical service and does not collect information for medical purposes. Our Terms & Conditions describe the Service’s wellness disclaimers and govern your use of it.

2. Our principles

  • Keep it on your device. The best way to protect your information is not to collect it. Tappy is built so that the personal things you record never need to reach a server we run. We do not run a server that stores your content.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Tappy is funded by optional purchases, not by data. There is nothing in Tappy that profiles you, follows you across apps or websites, or shows you advertising.
  • Only what a feature needs, only while you use it. Every device permission Tappy asks for powers a specific feature you tapped on, is used on your device, and can be turned off in iOS Settings without breaking the rest of the app.
  • Plain language. We have tried to write this policy so a normal person can read it. If anything is unclear, email us and we will explain, and we may improve the wording.
  • If we change something, we will tell you. Tappy will not start collecting new categories of information without updating this policy first and, where the law requires it, asking for your consent.

3. Information Tappy handles, and where it lives

This section lists every category of information Tappy works with. For each one we say where the data is stored and whether Pacific can see it. “On your device” means stored inside Tappy’s private storage on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch. “Your iCloud” means the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account, which only you (and Apple, under Apple’s iCloud terms) can access.

3.1 Content you create

When you use Tappy’s wellness and planning features, you may enter: mood or emotion selections; feelings, activities, and reasons you tag; journal text and the prompt you responded to; habit names, mottos, notes, and reminder times; to-do titles, notes, and subtasks; goals and goal updates; drawings; the name you give your Tappy character; the names you give custom Daily Adventure quests and saved outfits; Tappy’s bedtime schedule; and your customization preferences (theme colors, faces, sounds, haptic intensity, and similar settings).

What you write or draw during the Daily Adventure gratitude quest is not saved. It exists only on screen while the quest is open and is discarded when you close it; Tappy records only that you completed the quest.

Where it lives: on your device, and in your iCloud if you have iCloud enabled for Tappy. Can Pacific see it? No. Tappy does not upload this content to Pacific servers, and Pacific has no ability to read your private iCloud database. We do not use it to train models, build profiles, or for advertising.

3.2 Game and progress data

Tappy keeps track of gameplay and care mechanics: tap counts, Sparks, Self-Care Streaks and Streak Freezes, energy and hunger, wardrobe and equipped items, badges, Daily Adventure quests and chest history, gift box history, timers, and arcade scores.

Where it lives: on your device and, for most of it, in your iCloud so that it syncs between your devices. Tappy also keeps a backup copy of your progress in your iCloud account, described in Section 6.2. The Apple Watch app keeps its own settings and tap counts on the watch. Can Pacific see it? No, except for scores and achievements you choose to submit to Game Center, which appear on Apple’s leaderboards (see Section 3.6).

3.3 Apple Health data

If you turn on Apple Health integration, Tappy writes Mindful Minutes from breathing and meditation sessions and, with Tappy Plus, State of Mind entries derived from your mood entries into the Health app. Section 7 describes this in detail. Can Pacific see it? No. Health data stays in Apple Health on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3.4 Purchase information

Tappy Plus (monthly, annual, or lifetime), the optional “Buy Me Coffee” tip, and any legacy one-time packs are sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details and Apple Account information under Apple’s privacy policy. We never receive your name, email address, or payment card details from a purchase.

To confirm which purchases are active and to restore them on a new device, Tappy uses a subscription-management provider, currently RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives and stores, on our behalf:

  • a randomly generated anonymous identifier created by the RevenueCat software on your device (it is not your Apple ID, name, email, or advertising identifier, and Tappy does not link it to anything that identifies you);
  • your App Store receipt and the transaction details derived from it (product identifiers, purchase and renewal dates, prices, refund or cancellation status);
  • basic technical context: platform, iOS version, Tappy version, country, and locale, plus first-seen and last-seen timestamps.

Tappy does not send RevenueCat your name, email, phone number, IP address as a stored attribute, advertising identifier, or any content from the app. Like any internet service, RevenueCat’s servers necessarily see your device’s IP address while the app is communicating with them. RevenueCat acts as our service provider (a “processor”), may use the data only to provide its service to us, and describes its practices in its privacy policy. We can see this anonymous purchase record in RevenueCat’s dashboard; it does not tell us who you are.

4. What Tappy does not do

Because privacy policies usually describe what a company collects, it is worth stating clearly what Tappy does not do:

  • No user accounts, passwords, or login. There is no Pacific account to create, and we hold no profile of you.
  • No third-party advertising, ad networks, or advertising SDKs. Tappy has never shown third-party ads and does not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) or App Tracking Transparency, because there is nothing to track.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDKs (no Firebase, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Meta, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch, Sentry, or similar).
  • No “tracking” as defined by Apple or by the CCPA: we do not link data about you across apps or websites, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • No selling, renting, or licensing of personal information, and no sharing of health-related information for any purpose other than the ones you turn on yourself.
  • No collection of your name, birthday, gender, age, precise location history, contacts, photos, or microphone audio.
  • No Pacific server that stores your content. Your mood entries, journal, habits, to-dos, and drawings never leave your device and your own iCloud account.
  • No reading of your other Apple Health data. Tappy asks for read access to the two Health types it writes only so it can find and delete its own entries when you delete them in Tappy.
  • No monitoring. Nobody at Pacific reviews, screens, or has access to what you write.

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing, our bases for the information we actually receive are: performance of our contract with you (purchase records, remote configuration, support), our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service and understanding aggregate performance (which we have balanced against your rights), and compliance with legal obligations. Processing that happens only on your device or in your iCloud account is carried out by the software under your control, and Apple Health, camera, location, motion, local-network, and Face ID features run only after you grant the relevant iOS permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

6. Where your data lives, and who can see it

6.1 On your device

Tappy stores your content in its private app container on your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. iOS protects this storage with the device’s built-in encryption when your device is locked with a passcode. Other apps cannot read it. Anyone who can unlock your device can open Tappy, so the optional journal lock (Section 11) is there for shared devices.

6.2 In your iCloud

If iCloud is enabled for Tappy (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud → Tappy), your content and progress are also stored in the private iCloud database tied to your Apple Account, which is how Tappy syncs between your iPhone and iPad. Apple encrypts this data in transit and at rest and, depending on your iCloud settings such as Advanced Data Protection, may also protect it end-to-end. This storage is part of your iCloud account and your iCloud storage quota; it is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and the iCloud terms. Pacific cannot read, export, or delete your private iCloud data. You can delete Tappy’s synced data using Apple’s settings, except for the progress backup described next.

Progress backup. Tappy also keeps a small backup of your game progress in iCloud key-value storage, a separate part of your iCloud account, so that Sparks and items you have earned are not lost if syncing runs into a problem. The backup holds your Sparks and friendship level, Self-Care Streak and Streak Freezes, perfect days, owned and equipped wardrobe items, saved outfits and the names you give them, badges, gift box history and one-time gifts already claimed, lifetime tap count, the date of your last Daily Adventure, and your Daily Adventure setup, including the names of any custom quests you write. It does not contain mood entries, journal text, habits, to-dos, goals, drawings, or Apple Health data. Like the rest of your iCloud data, it is stored by Apple and Pacific cannot see it. Tappy updates the backup whenever your progress changes, so deleting a custom quest or saved outfit in Tappy also removes its name from the backup. iOS does not list this storage in iCloud settings, and deleting Tappy’s data from iCloud there does not remove it, so if you reinstall Tappy on the same Apple Account, this progress can come back (see Section 12).

6.3 Widgets, Live Activities, and the lock screen

If you add Tappy widgets, a small snapshot (today’s mood selection, streaks, habit names and progress, to-do titles, Sparks, and adventure progress) is shared with the widget through a private shared container on your device so it can be displayed on your home or lock screen. Anyone who can see your screen can see the widget. Remove the widget if you do not want that information visible.

When a timed quest or Focus timer is running, Tappy may show a countdown with the session’s name as a Live Activity on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. It is created and updated on your device, not by a Pacific server, and it ends when the session stops. You can turn Live Activities off in iOS Settings → Tappy.

6.4 Notifications

Reminders can show your character’s name and habit or to-do names on your lock screen. You can control what appears in iOS Settings → Notifications → Tappy (for example by turning off lock-screen previews), or turn reminders off inside Tappy.

6.5 Apple Watch

The Tappy Apple Watch app runs independently. It stores its settings and tap counts on the watch and can submit scores to Game Center. It does not access your mood journal or Apple Health data.

6.6 Shared devices and Family Sharing

Tappy has no separate user profiles. If you share a device, everyone who uses it shares the same Tappy data. If you share an Apple Account, iCloud data is shared as well. Purchases made under Family Sharing are governed by Apple’s Family Sharing rules.

7. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Apple Health integration is off by default. You can turn it on in Tappy’s Settings or from the breathing screen, at which point iOS asks you to grant Tappy access to specific Health data types. You can change or revoke that access at any time in the Health app (Profile → Apps & Services → Tappy, or Privacy → Apps → Tappy, depending on your iOS version).

7.1 What Tappy writes

  • Mindful Minutes (the Mindful Session type): the start and end time of breathing and meditation sessions you complete in Tappy.
  • State of Mind (iOS 18 and later, with Tappy Plus and only if you turn this option on separately): a momentary-emotion entry created from each mood entry you log, containing the emotion’s pleasantness, associated labels, and time. Your journal text is not written to Health.

7.2 What Tappy reads

Tappy requests read access only to the two data types above, and uses it for one purpose: when you delete a mood entry or session in Tappy, Tappy looks up the matching entry that Tappy itself created in Health so it can delete that entry too. Tappy does not read any other Health data, does not build insights from Health data, and does not import Health data written by other apps or devices.

7.3 Our commitments

  • Health data stays in Apple Health on your device. Tappy never transmits it to Pacific, RevenueCat, iCloud (outside of Apple’s own Health sync, which you control), or anyone else.
  • We never use Health data for advertising, marketing, analytics, data mining, or any purpose other than the feature you turned on.
  • We never sell Health data or share it with data brokers, advertisers, information resellers, or any third party.
  • We comply with Apple’s HealthKit developer guidelines and App Store Review requirements for health data.

To delete what Tappy wrote to Health, open the Health app, go to Tappy’s app page, and choose “Delete All Data from Tappy,” or delete individual samples in the relevant Health category.

9. Consumer health data notice

Some laws, including Washington’s My Health My Data Act, Nevada’s consumer health data law, and Connecticut’s privacy law, give special protection to “consumer health data,” meaning information that could reveal your past, present, or future physical or mental health. Mood entries, journal text, mindfulness sessions, and State of Mind entries could be consumer health data. This section is our consumer health data privacy notice. It applies to everyone, wherever you live.

9.1 Categories of consumer health data Tappy processes, and why

  • Mood and emotion selections, feelings, activities, and reasons you tag — processed on your device to show your mood history, charts, and insights to you.
  • Journal text and prompts — processed on your device so you can write, read, search, and export your own reflections.
  • Habit, goal, to-do, and custom Daily Adventure quest entries (which may describe wellness routines) — processed on your device to track them for you.
  • Breathing, meditation, grounding, and Feelscape session activity — processed on your device to run the session and, if you turn on Apple Health, to record Mindful Minutes in your Health app.
  • State of Mind entries — created in your Health app from your mood entries only if you have Tappy Plus and turn that option on.

All processing of consumer health data happens on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your personal iCloud account. Pacific does not collect, receive, store, or infer consumer health data on its own systems.

9.2 Sources

The only source of consumer health data in Tappy is you: what you enter, select, or do in the App.

9.3 Sharing and third parties

Pacific does not share consumer health data with any third party, affiliate, or service provider, and does not sell it. Consumer health data leaves Tappy only when you direct it: to Apple Health on your device after you grant permission, to your own iCloud account if you enable iCloud, or to recipients you choose when you export or share. We do not process consumer health data for advertising, and we do not use geofencing of any kind.

9.4 Your rights

You have the right to know what consumer health data is processed, to access it, to withdraw consent, and to have it deleted. Because this data is on your device and in your iCloud, you exercise these rights directly: view it in Tappy, export it as a CSV, revoke Apple Health access in the Health app, and delete it using the steps in Section 12. If you believe Pacific holds any consumer health data about you, or you want to make a request or appeal a decision, email cameron@pacific.dev with the subject line “Consumer Health Data Request.” We will respond within the time the applicable law requires (45 days in most states, extendable once if permitted), and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response; if your appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

10. Children

Tappy is a general-audience app that includes health and wellness topics; its age rating is shown on its App Store listing. Tappy is not directed to children under 13. Because families and children may still use it, Tappy is built so that we collect almost nothing from anyone, whatever their age, and this section explains exactly what that means for children.

10.1 What we collect from children

A child’s mood entries, journal, habits, to-dos, drawings, character, and progress stay on the device and in the family’s iCloud account. We never receive them. The only information Pacific receives from any Tappy user, regardless of age, is (a) the anonymous purchase record described in Section 3.4, and (b) the contents of any email someone chooses to send us. We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, audio, precise location, or contact details through the App, and we do not let children make personal information public through Tappy: nearby multiplayer shows only a random player name, and Game Center is operated by Apple under its own parental controls.

10.2 The purchase identifier

The random identifier in the purchase record is the only persistent identifier associated with Tappy. We use it solely to support Tappy’s internal operations: validating purchases, restoring them on a new device, processing renewals and refunds, and preventing fraud. We make sure it is used for nothing else by never linking it to a name, email address, or other identifier; never using it to contact anyone, build a profile, or serve advertising; and limiting our subscription-management provider, RevenueCat, by contract to processing it only to provide its service to us.

10.3 Emails from children

If we learn that an email came from a child under 13, we use the child’s email address only to reply once to that message. We then delete the message and the address, and we do not contact the child again or add the address to any list.

10.4 Parents’ rights

A parent or guardian may at any time ask us to review any personal information we hold about their child, to delete it, and to refuse any further collection or use of it. Email cameron@pacific.dev with “Parent Request” in the subject line, or write to us at the address in Section 20. Because we hold no names or accounts, we may ask for details that let us find the information, such as the Apple order ID of a purchase or the email address the child used. We do not require a child to provide more information than Tappy needs in order to use any feature. Everything else is on the family’s device and iCloud, where a parent can review or delete it directly using Sections 11 and 12.

We do not disclose children’s personal information to anyone except RevenueCat as our service provider (Section 10.2) and where required by law (Section 8).

10.5 Parental controls

If a child uses Tappy on a device a parent or guardian manages, Apple’s Family Sharing, Screen Time, Ask to Buy, and Game Center parental controls apply. Parents and guardians are responsible for deciding whether Tappy’s wellness, journaling, Apple Health, and multiplayer features are appropriate for their child. If you believe we have received personal information from a child under 13 in a way this section does not describe, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Users in the European Union may be subject to a higher age of digital consent (up to 16) under national law; the same approach applies.

11. Your choices and controls

  • Device permissions. Grant or revoke camera, location, motion, local network, Face ID, notifications, and Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Tappy, or in the Health app.
  • iCloud sync. Turn iCloud on or off for Tappy in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud → Tappy. Turning it off keeps your data on your device; deleting the iCloud copy is described in Section 12.
  • Apple Health. Turn Mindful Minutes and State of Mind sync on or off inside Tappy, and manage or revoke permissions in the Health app.
  • Journal lock. Turn on Face ID / Touch ID / passcode protection for the Mood Journal in Tappy’s mood-diary settings. It is off by default.

  • Nearby multiplayer. Nearby players see only a random player name. To turn nearby multiplayer off entirely, deny the Local Network permission in iOS Settings → Tappy.
  • Export. Export your mood journal as a CSV file (columns: date, mood, feelings, reasons, prompt, journal) from Tappy’s mood-diary settings at any time.
  • Reduce Haptics. Not a privacy setting, but available in Tappy’s settings if vibration bothers you. Tappy’s animation stays on when iOS Reduce Motion is enabled.

12. Deleting your data

Because your data is in your hands, you can delete it without asking us. Here is everything, step by step.

  1. Delete individual entries. Swipe or tap to delete mood entries, journal entries, habits, to-dos, goals, and drawings inside Tappy. Deleting a mood entry also removes the matching State of Mind entry Tappy wrote to Health, if that sync is on. The Habits screen offers “Delete All Habits.”
  2. Delete everything on your device. Delete the Tappy app (touch and hold the icon → Remove App → Delete App, or iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Tappy → Delete App). “Offload App” does not delete your data. Delete the Watch app from your Apple Watch as well.
  3. Delete your iCloud copy. Deleting the app does not remove data already synced to iCloud. Go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or “Saved to iCloud” → Manage) → Tappy → Delete Data from iCloud. Labels vary slightly by iOS version. If you reinstall Tappy without doing this, your data will come back. This step does not remove the progress backup described in Section 6.2, which iOS does not let you delete from Settings: if you reinstall Tappy on the same Apple Account, your Sparks, wardrobe, badges, Daily Adventure setup, and tap count can return. To remove the custom quest names and outfit names stored in that backup, delete those quests and saved outfits in Tappy before you delete the app.
  4. Delete Health data Tappy wrote. Open the Health app → your profile → Apps & Services (or Privacy → Apps) → Tappy → Delete All Data from Tappy. You can also turn off Tappy’s write permission there.
  5. Game Center. Scores and achievements are held by Apple as part of your Game Center profile and are managed through your Apple Account; Pacific cannot delete them.
  6. Cancel your subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel Tappy Plus. Cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tappy.
  7. Ask us to delete the anonymous purchase record. Email cameron@pacific.dev. Because the record is anonymous, we will need something that lets us find it, such as the order ID from your Apple purchase receipt; we may not be able to locate it otherwise. Deleting it does not affect your Apple purchase history, but it may make restoring Tappy Plus harder (you can still restore through Apple). Apple keeps its own transaction records under its policy.

14. Security

The most effective security measure in Tappy is architectural: we do not hold your content, so it cannot be exposed by a breach of our systems. Beyond that:

  • Data on your device is protected by iOS Data Protection and is encrypted when your device is locked with a passcode. Keep a passcode set and iOS up to date.
  • Data in your iCloud is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest, with additional end-to-end protection available through Apple’s Advanced Data Protection.
  • All network communication from Tappy (to Apple and to RevenueCat) uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • The journal lock is an access control inside the App. It does not add a separate layer of encryption, and it can be satisfied by your device passcode. Pacific cannot bypass, reset, or recover it.
  • Access to the RevenueCat dashboard and to our support mailbox is limited to the people who need it to run Tappy and is protected by strong authentication.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting information we hold about you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
