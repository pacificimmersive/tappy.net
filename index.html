Daily Adventure

Your routine, turned into an adventure.

Create your own Daily Adventure from self-care quests, then complete it each day. Tappy cheers you on with a treasure compass on every quest.

  • Choose from 25+ quests: breathe, fidget, journal, drink water, step outside, move your body, practice gratitude, reach out to a friend, brush your teeth, wind down, or write your own.
  • Arrange them in any order and set a daily reminder to build a routine that sticks.
  • Finish your adventure to open a treasure chest of Sparks. The bigger the adventure, the bigger the reward.

FAQ

Questions? Tappy's got answers.

What is Tappy?

Tappy is a self-care pet and fidget app for iPhone and Apple Watch. Tappy levels up as you destress, stay focused, and build a routine that's all yours. It includes a Daily Adventure routine builder, 25+ haptic fidgets, breathing exercises, meditations, Feelscapes, a mood journal, habit tracker, planner, goals, a focus timer, and arcade games.

Is Tappy free?

Yes. Tappy is free to download on the App Store, with optional in-app purchases including Tappy Plus.

What's new in Tappy 6.0?

Tappy is now a self-care pet who grows with you. It's our biggest update ever:

  • Daily Adventure: build your own routine from 25+ self-care quests and open a treasure chest of Sparks.
  • Take care of Tappy: pet, feed, hug, play, and tuck Tappy in, and fill the Care Bar for a daily surprise gift.
  • Sparks and the Shop: spend Sparks on hats, outfits, toys, and colors.
  • New scenes: the beach, a farm, a castle, outer space, and more, with visitors to discover in the Field Guide.
  • Bedtime, Goals, badges, and friendship levels.
  • A Daily Adventure widget, plus Live Activities for timed quests.
What are Sparks?

Sparks are rewards you collect for everything you finish in Tappy, like quests, focus sessions, and your Daily Adventure. Spend them in the Shop on hats, glasses, outfits, props, toys, colors, and scenes to level Tappy up.

Is Tappy good for fidgeting and stimming?

Tappy gives your hands something to do and your mind a place to settle, whether you're stimming at your desk, waiting in line, or riding out a stressful moment. Each of the 25+ fidgets has its own haptic feel, and breathing exercises, meditations, and a 5 senses exercise are there whenever you want a moment to slow down.

Tappy is a fidget and self-care tool and is not a substitute for professional care.

Can I use Tappy's fidgets silently?

Yes. Turn sound on for satisfying audio, or off for a silent, discreet stim anywhere. You can also pin a haptic calming strip that floats over the app so it's always within reach.

Does Tappy work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Tappy on Apple Watch gives you a quick, discreet fidget on your wrist. On iPhone, Tappy also supports Apple Health mindful minutes, Home Screen widgets, and Live Activities for timed quests.

Is my mood journal private?

Your journal entries stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and you can lock them with Face ID or Touch ID. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I go back to the original Tappy?

Yes. On the home screen, tap More, then Switch to Classic Tappy.
