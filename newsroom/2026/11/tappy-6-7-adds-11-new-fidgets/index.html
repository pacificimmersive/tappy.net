Tappy 6.7 adds 11 new fidgets, from slime to fireworks

Squish slime, crack wax, cut soap, set off fireworks and more, plus a brand-new puzzle game, Shelf Sort.

Media

Tappy 6.7 is available today as a free update on the App Store. It’s packed with 11 new fidgets to squish, stretch, crack, cut, snap and sort, a new puzzle game called Shelf Sort, and easier ways to browse the whole fidget collection.

Squish, stretch and pop

Slime is a glossy blob to squish, stretch and pop. Mix your own colors and mix-ins, then save your favorite batches to your shelf. Putty is 3D thinking putty you can poke, stretch and snap, with air pockets to pop and styles like heat-changing, glitter and magnetic. And Squishy Shapes is a soft, see-through cube that wobbles back after every squeeze, with shapes and fillings to choose from.

Crack, cut and crumble

Wax Cracking is a tray of hard wax to press down on and feel crack into crunchy shards. Pick your own colors and finishes for the next tray. Soap Cutting lets you slice, shave and cube a bar of crackly soap, then crush the pieces into crumbs.

Light up the sky, then say cheese

Fireworks turns the screen into a night sky. Tap anywhere to set off a firework that bursts right where you touched, with a boom you can feel, or drag a spark across the sky and let it go. Camera is a detailed 3D camera you can spin around. Press the shutter for a crisp click and wind the 35mm, or switch to Instant and watch prints whir out.

A place for everything

Sorting Tray is a pile of glossy hearts, stars and gems to drag into their trays, sorted by color, by shape or any way you like. Each piece clicks into place, and there’s no timer and no score. In Rainbow Sort, swap mixed-up color tiles around until they flow into a smooth rainbow, with a soft chime as each one lands. Play it as a single line, a small board or a big board.

Meet Shelf Sort

Shelf Sort is a brand-new sorting puzzle in the Arcade, with boosters for tricky levels and a laid-back Zen mode. The first 30 levels are free.

Play, jangle and type

Piano lets you play multiple instruments, and you can pinch to resize the keys. Keychain is a ring of keys that clink like the real thing. Jangle them anytime, hold the ring and yank a key right off, or add and remove keys. And the Mechanical Keyboard now has an eight-key starter keypad with RGB lighting.

Find your favorites faster

The Fidget tab now puts the newest fidgets in a spotlight at the top, so you can try one in a tap or jump to it in the grid. Switch to the list to sort every fidget A to Z, by what’s newly added, by what you open most or by category, and filter by type, like Clicky, Satisfying and Sorting.

Availability

  • Tappy 6.7 is available today as a free update on the App Store for iPhone, and requires iOS 16.6 or later.
  • Tappy is free to download. Rainbow Sort and Keychain are free for everyone, and the first 30 levels of Shelf Sort are free.
  • Slime, Wax Cracking, Soap Cutting, Putty, Squishy Shapes, Fireworks, Camera, Sorting Tray and Piano are included with Tappy Plus, and you can try each one first.

About Tappy

Tappy is a fidget and self-care pet app for iPhone, where every tap, click and swipe comes with satisfying sounds, haptics and animations. Tappy the pet levels up as you play, breathe, journal and build habits.
